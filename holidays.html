Festivals & Holidays

Annual celebrations across Azeroth trigger substantial political, economic, and magical shifts — dictating diplomatic trade windows, troop mobilization drives, and spiritual convergences.

13 celebrations 7–16 days each 137 festival days per year

The Festival Year

Dates are given as month and day on the Azerothian calendar. Only Highspring, Harvestide, and Amberfall carry no major celebration.

Celebrations in Detail

Lunar Festival

Month 1 · First Dawn, Days 15 – 28 14 days

AllianceHorde Night ElvesTauren

Ancestral spirit activity spikes worldwide; Elders award Lunar Coins; Elune blessings grant temporary stamina and spell resistance; minor faction ceasefires enforced at ancestral grounds.

Love is in the Air

Month 2 · Awakening, Days 7 – 20 14 days

All factionsGoblins HumansOrcs

Massive trade surges in chemical fragrances; Crown Chemical Co. infiltrations trigger biohazard containment protocols in major cities.

Noblegarden

Month 3 · Sunreach, Days 15 – 21 7 days

AllianceHorde

Light-infused fertility rituals; egg-sourcing logistics consume agricultural supply lines; public morale buffs in human and blood elf settlements.

Kosh'harg Festival (Spring)

Month 3 · Sunreach, Days 21 – 27 7 days

Orcish ClansMag'har

Total cessation of intra-orcish violence; clan elders converge at Oshu'gun (Nagrand) or Durotar; ancestral communion grants clan blessings; Om'riggor rites initiated for young warriors.

Night Elf Summer Solstice

Month 5 · Solstice Peak, Days 13 – 19 7 days

Night ElvesHighborne Druidic Orders

Peak ley-line siphon efficiency; moonwell recharge rates double; Sentinels rotate defensive deployments along the Ashenvale borders.

Sunstrider Rites

Month 5 · Solstice Peak, Days 25 – 31 7 days

Blood ElvesHigh Elves

Commemoration of Dath'Remar; the Sunwell is recalibrated and magical intake regulated across Quel'Thalas; arcane spellcasting efficiency boosted for Thalassian races.

Midsummer Fire Festival

Month 6 · Hearthfire, Days 10 – 24 15 days

All factions

Elemental Fire spirits rage; bonfires stoked across all territories provide stamina buffs; Ahune's forces attempt frost invasions in the Slave Pens.

Harvest Festival

Month 9 · Shadowrise, Days 5 – 11 7 days

AllianceHorde

Logistics lines carry massive grain shipments to urban centers; memorial tributes paid to heroes (Uther Lightbringer, Grom Hellscream); food storage drives for the coming winter.

Kosh'harg Festival (Autumn)

Month 9 · Shadowrise, Days 21 – 27 7 days

Orcish ClansMag'har

Second annual equinox convergence; trade of game and weapons; formal council of chieftains; integration rites for newly arrived clans.

Brewfest

Month 9, Day 25 → Month 10, Day 8 14 days

AllianceHorde DwarvesOrcs

Mass supply logistics of hops and grains; Dark Iron Dwarf invasions target iron and brewing infrastructure outside Ironforge and Orgrimmar.

Hallow's End

Month 10, Day 18 → Month 11, Day 1 15 days

ForsakenAlliance Horde

Commemorates the Forsaken liberation; Wickerman burnings in Tirisfal and Southshore; the Headless Horseman unleashes fire attacks on low-level settlements; Fel/Death magic affinity rises.

Ironforge Foundry Rites

Month 11 · Deepwinter, Days 10 – 16 7 days

DwarvesGnomes

Re-ignition and purification of the Great Forge; titan-artifact resonance tests; peak iron and steel production drive across Khaz Modan.

Feast of Winter Veil

Month 12 · Veilfall, Days 15 – 30 16 days

All factionsSmokywood Pastures

Greatfather Winter visits the capital cities; diplomatic trade truces active in neutral zones; festive food distribution strains transport networks.
