Core rules

Travel Rules

Survival and navigation for wild exploration across Azeroth — overland travel runs on a 6-mile hex grid with an 8-hour daily march.

4 daily phases 3 travel paces d20 encounter check

Traveling through Azeroth is rarely a peaceful stroll down the King's Road. Whether braving the toxic bogs of Dustwallow, the freezing winds of Frostfire Ridge, or the blighted soil of the Plaguelands, wilderness travel requires preparation, teamwork, and vigilance.

The Travel Phase Loop

Every day on the road follows the same four-step procedure, in order.

  1. Determine pace

    The party selects a Fast, Normal, or Slow pace for the march.

  2. Assign roles

    Each player chooses one travel activity — Scout, Hunter, Pioneer, and more.

  3. Roll environment

    The GM rolls 1d6 on the current zone's weather table for the day's hazards and DC modifiers.

  4. Check for encounters

    Roll a d20 for wilderness events. Encounters trigger on 18–20.

Wilderness Hazards

Unlike standard high-fantasy travel, the wilds of Azeroth carry environmental corruption, elemental instability, and hostile factions.

Fel & Blight contamination

Unpurified water or food gathered in corrupted biomes requires a DC 13 Constitution saving throw to avoid disease or exhaustion.

Resting constraints

A Long Rest cannot be taken in hazardous or unfortified wilderness without a successful Camping / Fortification check by the party. Resting in a Harsh or Deadly biome without protective gear, shelter, or magical protection prevents recovering levels of Exhaustion during a Long Rest.

Daily exhaustion check

At the end of each travel day, every character makes a Constitution saving throw against the zone baseline DC + daily weather modifier. On a failure, the character gains 1 level of Exhaustion. Clearing it requires a night at a Safe Spot — or a bard with a good Motivate roll.

GM Checklist — Per Travel Day

  • Track rations & water — 1 ration & 1 gallon of water consumed per character.
  • Check exhaustion — did the party travel over 8 hours today? (DC 10 + 1 per extra hour Con check.)
  • Weather shift — roll 1d6 on the active zone's weather chart in the Biome Compendium.
