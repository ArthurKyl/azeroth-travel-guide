Calendar & Moons

How time is tracked on Azeroth — the ADP epoch, twelve solar months, the seven-day week, and the two moons whose cycles drive tides, ley-lines, and moonwell recharge.

The ADP Epoch

Temporal tracking across Azerothian societies relies on the After the Dark Portal (ADP) and Before the Dark Portal (BDP) epoch system.

Year 0

The opening of the rift between Azeroth and Draenor marks Year 0 ADP. Eras preceding this pivot point are numbered in reverse as BDP; everything after increments forward.

The campaign present

Under this framework, the onset of the Northrend campaign during the Wrath of the Lich King occurs in Year 27 ADP — see the Historical Timeline.

The solar year

The standardized solar year spans exactly 365 days across twelve months of fixed length, structured around the equinoxes and solstices. The week runs on a continuous 7-day cycle.

The Two Moons

Azeroth is orbited by two celestial bodies: the primary moon, the White Lady (also known as Lady Forlorn or Mu'sha), and the secondary moon, the Blue Child.

Syzygy — The Double Full Moon

Syzygy is the night both moons hit their peak together — White Lady day 15 and Blue Child day 46 falling on the same date. The two cycles resonate every 182 days (26 × 7 = 182, and 91 × 2 = 182), which is exactly 26 weeks. Since 2 × 182 = 364 and a third would need 546, a 365-day year always holds exactly two.

146 Solstice Peak 26 First syzygy of 27 ADP
328 Deepwinter 24 Second syzygy of 27 ADP

The slip

The year runs one day longer than two syzygy intervals (365 − 364 = 1), so the pair falls one day earlier each year: Solstice Peak 25 and Deepwinter 23 in 28 ADP, Solstice Peak 24 and Deepwinter 22 in 29 ADP, and so on. It takes 182 years for the alignment to walk the calendar once — which is exactly the sort of thing Kirin Tor astronomers keep tables for.

Arcano-lunar tides

During syzygy, Azeroth experiences arcano-lunar tides:

  • Ley-line throughput surges by 300%.
  • Druids and Priests of Elune experience doubled mana recovery rates.
  • Planar boundaries between Azeroth, the Emerald Dream, and the Spirit Realm thin significantly.

Both moons can show a full face on the same night without a syzygy — the Blue Child's Full Child phase alone runs 19 days. Only the coinciding peak days count.
