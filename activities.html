Core rules

Travel Activities

Each day on the march, every player selects one travel activity. Choose wisely — the wilds do not forgive idle hands.

8 activities 1 per character per day

The Daily Loop

  1. Pace selection

    The party chooses their marching speed for the day.

  2. Biome exhaustion check

    Roll a daily Constitution or Survival check against the current biome DC. Failure: gain 1 level of Exhaustion. Clearing it requires a night at a Safe Spot (settlement, tavern, outpost) or a successful Motivate action.

  3. Assign activities

    Each player selects 1 travel activity for the day from the eight below.

Activity stacking

Two or more players can perform the same activity:

  • Solo (double output): both players roll separately. Each roll resolves individually, so the party can gain double the rewards — two separate rolls for Hunting rations or Scavenging loot.
  • Helping (safety first): one player rolls as primary while the second assists. The primary rolls with advantage, but the activity produces only a single reward on a success.

The 8 Activities

Scouting

Keep eyes on the horizon to spot threats, ambushes, or points of interest early.

Wisdom (Perception) or Dexterity (Stealth)

  • Success: the party cannot be surprised during encounters today, and hidden points of interest (ruins, caves, outposts) in adjacent hexes are revealed.
  • Failure: standard passive Perception applies.

Hunting

Track and hunt wild game to feed the party.

Wisdom (Survival)

  • Success: yields 1d4 + Wisdom modifier rations.
  • Failure: find no game.
  • Natural 1: disturb a wild predator or minor hazard.

Gathering

Harvest useful flora or remedies along the trail. Choose your approach before rolling.

Survival or Medicine

  • Survival (rations): yields 1d4 edible plant or berry rations.
  • Medicine (remedies): finds 1 daily dose of healing herbs — restores 1d4 + 2 HP when consumed.

Cover Tracks

Hide party footprints and signs of passage.

Dexterity (Stealth) or Wisdom (Survival)

  • Success: the party's trail becomes invisible to tracking. If currently stalked or chased, the party slips away and breaks pursuit.
  • Failure: the party leaves standard tracks.

Pray / Commune

Commune with your deity, ancestors, Elune, the Holy Light, or the Loa. Gain a daily boon lasting 24 hours based on your d20 roll.

Intelligence (Religion) or Charisma (Persuasion)

1–9No response.
10–14Light Blessing — gain temporary HP equal to your proficiency bonus.
15–19Ancestor's Grace — advantage on your first attack roll or saving throw today.
20+Elemental Favor — gain +1 AC for the day.

Motivate

Use inspiring war chants, stories, or music to keep spirits high despite physical strain.

Charisma (Performance or Persuasion)

  • Success (DC = biome DC): remove 1 level of Exhaustion gained today from 1 target player.
  • Natural 20: rally the entire party — remove 1 level of Exhaustion from all party members.
  • Failure: no effect.

Pioneer

Blaze a cleaner trail through dense brush, test ice, or clear obstacles ahead of the march.

Wisdom (Survival) or Strength (Athletics)

10–14Lowers the daily biome exhaustion DC by −2 for the whole party.
15+Lowers the daily biome exhaustion DC by −4 for the whole party.
Nat 20The entire party automatically succeeds their biome exhaustion check for the day.
FailThe pioneer takes a −2 penalty on their own biome exhaustion check today due to strain.

Scavenging

Search old battlefields, ruined camps, dry creek beds, and debris for items of value.

Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Perception)

1–9You ain't getting shit.
10–14Find minor scraps and trinkets worth 1d6 gold.
15–19Find valuable salvage worth 3d6 gold.
20+Find a rare trade good, intact gem, or pouch worth 5d6 gold.
