Weather & Exhaustion Primer

Biome classes

DC 10Mild — temperate forests, pastoral plains, magical sanctuaries. Resources abound.
DC 14Harsh — jungles, deserts, swamps, tundras, and alpine crags.
DC 18Deadly — volcanic wastes, scourgelands, fel-shatters, glacial peaks.

Daily weather (1d6)

1–3Clear · +0 DC — standard visibility, pace, and resource use.
4–5Moderate · +2 DC — lightly obscured; ranged attack range halved; unpaved ground is difficult terrain.
6Severe · +4 DC — heavily obscured; disadvantage on Perception and ranged attacks; extreme heat or cold doubles resource depletion or deals periodic damage.

Hex navigation sequence

  1. Roll 1d6 for daily weather to set the DC modifier and active hazards.
  2. Determine travel pace — fast, normal, or slow.
  3. Wisdom (Survival) check to navigate hexes without marked roads.
  4. At day's end, every character makes a Constitution save vs. baseline + weather DC. Failure: 1 level of Exhaustion.
