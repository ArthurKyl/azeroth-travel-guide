Core rules

Settlement Downtime

When the party reaches a village or city, the march pauses and downtime begins. Every settlement — from Goldshire to Dalaran — offers the same six locations. Each day in town, every character commits to one of them.

6 locations every village & city 1 token per character per day

Downtime Rules

Safe Spot

Every settlement is a Safe Spot: a night's rest here clears Exhaustion gained on the road — no Camping or Fortification check needed. Stock up before heading back into the wilds.

Check DCs by disposition

DC 12Friendly — the party is welcome, or unknown but unremarkable.
DC 15Wary — suspicious locals, contested territory, or a bad reputation.
DC 18Hostile — enemy-aligned or occupied. Tread very carefully.

Reputation

Track Reputation per settlement. It rises through goodwill and coin, and falls through scandal and suspicion.

+1–2Friendly faces — small discounts, a free bed offered.
+3–4Trusted — better goods surface; locals volunteer information freely.
+5Local champion — quest hooks, safe haven, doors open everywhere.
< 0Watched → unwelcome — prices rise, tongues still, guards follow.

The Six Locations

Available in every village and city, regardless of region. Costs scale with settlement size — a copper-pinch hamlet versus a gilded capital.

The Tavern

Information, social lubrication & local rumors

Drink & Gossip Free

Charisma (Persuasion) or Intelligence (Investigation)

Success: discover a regional rumor, a lead on a nearby hex, or an unverified quest hint.

Buy a Round 5–20 gold · by settlement size

No check — automatic

Effect: loosen local tongues and earn an immediate +1 Reputation with the settlement. Any Drink & Gossip attempts here today are made with advantage.

The Town Square

Civic goodwill, open inquiries & local standing

Civic Favor Minor effort or small gift

No check — roleplay only

Effect: help push a cart, fix a fence, hand out candy — earn +1 Reputation with the locals without spending gold.

Mingle & Ask Free

Charisma (Persuasion) or Wisdom (Insight)

Success: learn about immediate civic needs, town notices, or public requests from everyday folk.

The Underbelly

High-risk secrets, black markets & illicit opportunities

Single check: Dexterity (Stealth), Charisma (Deception), or Intelligence (Investigation)

1–9You stand out. Nobody talks to you. Local eyes watch with suspicion — risk a minor Reputation penalty.
10–14Whispered secrets. Uncover hidden lore, secret faction activity, or a dark hint tied to an ongoing quest.
15+Risky mission. An off-the-books job offer: high reward, high threat, morally gray objectives.

The Scout Den

Wilderness preparation, local trappers & hexcrawl intel

Single check: Wisdom (Survival or Nature), or a flat d20

1–9Conflicting stories and bad advice. No bonus.
10–14−2 to biome exhaustion & navigation DCs for the next 2 days — hunters outline safe paths through the immediate hexes.
15–19−3 to biome exhaustion & navigation DCs for the next 3 days — trappers point out hidden animal trails and water sources.
20+−5 to biome exhaustion & navigation DCs for the next 3 days, and reveal 1 adjacent hex feature — detailed maps of hazard zones, landmarks, and monster nests.

Overlapping reductions don't stack — on days a Pioneer is also cutting the trail, apply the better of the two.

The Chapel

The Light, the Loa, Elune — every settlement keeps a shrine

Tithe for Cleansing 10–50 gold · by settlement size

No check — donation

Effect: an acolyte casts Lesser Restoration — cure one disease or poison, including Fel/Blight contamination picked up from corrupted food or water on the road.

Quiet Devotion Free

Intelligence (Religion) or Charisma (Persuasion)

Success: leave with steadied spirit — gain advantage on your first biome exhaustion save after leaving town.

The Provisioner

Rations, waterskins & gear for the biome ahead

Restock Supplies 5 silver per ration

No check — purchase

Effect: buy rations and refill water. Remember: 1 ration & 1 gallon per character per travel day — double water in extreme-heat weather.

Outfit for the Climate 10–25 gold per kit

No check — purchase

Effect: cold- or heat-weather gear. Without it, resting in a Harsh or Deadly biome recovers no Exhaustion — ask the shopkeep what the road ahead demands.
